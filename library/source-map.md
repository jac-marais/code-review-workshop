- `SRC-001`, [`Security Lens Review protocol`](../.agents/skills/security-lens-review/SKILL.md): derived workflow for isolated security lenses, central verification, root-risk deduplication, changed-line findings, and review-only operation. See [`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) for attribution.
- `SRC-002`, [`Robust Rules Review protocol`](../.agents/skills/review-robust-rules/SKILL.md): workflow for repository-context review against the Rules for Robust Software, candidate verification, deduplication, and changed-line findings.

These protocols establish workflow requirements. They do not prove successful use or establish broad empirical claims about review quality.
